Shenzhen is the center of China's electronics manufacturing and supplier ecosystem — from Huaqiangbei's component markets to the factories behind consumer electronics worldwide. Being based here, rather than sourcing remotely, gives ZENTO direct access to suppliers, faster quality control, and the ability to evaluate manufacturing partners in person.Shenzhen — Xitoyning elektronika ishlab chiqarish va yetkazib beruvchilar ekotizimining markazi — Huaqiangbei komponent bozorlaridan tortib butun dunyo iste'mol elektronikasi ortidagi zavodlargacha. Masofadan sourcing qilish o'rniga shu yerda joylashgan bo'lish ZENTO'ga yetkazib beruvchilarga bevosita kirish, tezroq sifat nazorati va ishlab chiqarish hamkorlarini shaxsan baholash imkonini beradi.

Business EcosystemTexnologiya va
Biznes Ekotizimi

ZENTO is a technology and business ecosystem building products, platforms and businesses across commerce, security/smart technology, and — increasingly — software and AI. It connects real operations in Uzbekistan with a growing base in Shenzhen, China. ZENTO is not just a marketplace, not just an AI startup, and not just a security-hardware company — those are businesses inside a broader ecosystem still being built.ZENTO — savdo, xavfsizlik/aqlli texnologiya va tobora ko'proq software va AI yo'nalishlarida mahsulot, platforma va bizneslar quruvchi texnologiya va biznes ekotizimi. U Uzbekistondagi real faoliyatni Shenzhen (Xitoy)dagi o'sib borayotgan baza bilan bog'laydi. ZENTO shunchaki marketplace, shunchaki AI startup yoki shunchaki xavfsizlik-hardware kompaniyasi emas — bular hali qurilayotgan kengroq ekotizim ichidagi bizneslar.
